I am a PhD candidate in **applied cryptography** at the
[Chair of Applied Cryptography](https://www.chaac.tf.fau.de) at
**Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)**, advised by
[Dominique Schröder](https://www.dominique-schroeder.de). My research applies
**formal methods** to **provable security**: I formalize cryptographic
primitives, protocols and security definitions, and study how they relate to
each other. Recent work covers anonymous authenticated key encapsulation,
atomic swaps and the automated analysis of message authentication codes.
